# SPACE RIDERS FIGHTER: Cosmic Championship

A free 16-bit one-on-one fighting game in the browser: https://sanyziahj.github.io/space-riders-fighter/

Shadow, Ronin, Drill Sergeant, ROS, Eyereenia and Spark fight for the Cosmic Championship against DeeJay and Cavendish. After the championship credits, someone is waiting at the Space Station Dock.

Six buttons (LP MP HP / LK MK HK), raygun blasts, motion and charge specials, Super Combos, throws, dizzies, best of 3 rounds, Training and Regular modes, save and continue, gamepads and touch controls. Let's Ride!
